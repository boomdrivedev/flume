## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: The fake agent's parts follow its scripts

The fake agent SHALL build each part its scripts give it and degrade
each other: send-back, guard and on-quiet unless scripted off, windows
unless scripted to report none, spent always, the mode once scripted to
read it back, carry once a carry is scripted, and own settings once
scripted to take them. It SHALL pass the agent seam's parts scenarios
under each of its harnesses, one offering no hooks among them (106,
174).

#### Scenario: The fake scripted as each harness scripts it

- **WHEN** the fake agent is scripted as its harness does by default, then to carry none, take no own settings, report no windows, and offer no send-back, guard or on-quiet
- **THEN** its parts build every part by default, and each script degrades its own part alone: carry, own settings, windows, and send-back, guard and on-quiet together (174)

#### Scenario: The fake as a new fake makes it

- **WHEN** a fake agent no test has scripted is asked its parts
- **THEN** send-back, guard, on-quiet, windows and spent are built, and the mode, carry and own settings degraded (174)
